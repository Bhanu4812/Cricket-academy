How Our Coaches Work

Great Coaching Starts With Seeing What Others Miss.

Our coaches combine technical knowledge, observation, communication and structured feedback to help players understand both what to improve and how to improve it.

Detail becomes development
  1. 01

    Observe

    Identify technical, tactical and physical patterns.

  2. 02

    Explain

    Make corrections clear and understandable.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Use purposeful drills to build repeatable skill.

  4. 04

    Apply

    Test improvements in realistic match situations.

“Players improve fastest when feedback is clear, training is purposeful and standards stay consistent.”

Arjun leads the academy with a player-first approach grounded in observation, honest communication and repeatable standards. Every session is designed to give players a clear understanding of where they are, what comes next and why the work matters.

His coaching connects sound technique with the decisions players must make under pressure. Rather than chasing short-term fixes, he builds adaptable cricketers who can recognise situations, trust their preparation and take responsibility for their performance.

Across every age group, Arjun asks for the same qualities: attention to detail, competitive intent and the discipline to improve one purposeful repetition at a time.
