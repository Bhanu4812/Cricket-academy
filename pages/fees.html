Payment Structure

Simple Payment Options. Clear Terms.

Review the available billing structures below. These are editable demo options and do not represent a live online payment facility.

Option 01 / Ongoing Training

Monthly

Billing Frequency
Once per month
Suitable Plan Type
Regular Foundation, Development or Performance training

A straightforward recurring schedule for players attending consistent weekly coaching. Confirm payment dates and inclusions directly with the academy.

Option 02 / Training Block

Term / Quarterly

Billing Frequency
Per academy term or quarter
Suitable Plan Type
Structured seasonal development programs

Groups scheduled coaching into a defined training period, helping families plan around a complete development block.

Option 03 / Defined Program

Program-Based

Billing Frequency
According to program schedule
Suitable Plan Type
Camps, clinics and fixed-duration pathways

Payment is aligned with a clearly defined program duration and its stated training inclusions.

Option 04 / Focused Session

One-Time Specialist Session

Billing Frequency
Per confirmed session
Suitable Plan Type
Specialist technical review or targeted coaching

Designed for a specific coaching need without suggesting an ongoing subscription or recurring commitment.

Payment availability may depend on the selected program.
